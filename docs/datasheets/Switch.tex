\datasheet{Switch}{A five-port switch of the network on chip, one per virtual channel.}

\dsfacts{\code{lib/parts/src/bus/noc/switch.rs}}{\code{txhdl\_parts::bus::noc::switch::Switch}}%
{\code{//docs:noc}}

\subsection*{Function}

A switch sits at column \code{X} and row \code{Y} of a lattice. It has
a two-way link on each of its four sides and a fifth port, the exit,
where something that is not a node attaches. Its inputs are
\code{n\_in}, \code{s\_in}, \code{w\_in}, \code{e\_in} and
\code{x\_in}, and its outputs are \code{n\_out}, \code{s\_out},
\code{w\_out}, \code{e\_out} and \code{x\_out}. Each port passes one
\code{Pkt}, a beat of one AXI channel addressed to a node. The switch
routes in dimension order, X before Y. A \code{Node} is two switches,
one per virtual channel.

\subsection*{Behaviour}

\begin{itemize}
\item The switch holds no state. The port a packet leaves by is a
function of the packet's \code{dx} and \code{dy} and of \code{X} and
\code{Y}.
\item The switch compares coordinates at \code{CW}, 8 bits. It sends a
packet east when \code{dx} is above \code{X}, and west when \code{dx}
is below \code{X}. In the right column, it sends the packet south when
\code{dy} is above \code{Y}, and north when \code{dy} is below
\code{Y}. At its own node it sends the packet out of the exit.
\item Each cycle the switch works out, for every input with a packet,
the port that packet wants and whether that port is ready. An input
whose port is full does not hold up the others.
\item Of the inputs that can move, the switch takes one per cycle, by
fixed priority: north, south, west, east, and the exit last.
\item The switch sends the packet it takes unchanged.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Verification}

\begin{itemize}
\item The switch has no test of its own. The three tests of the
network in \code{lib/parts/src/bus/noc.rs} run it inside four nodes:
\code{a\_burst\_crosses\_the\_lattice\_and\_its\_answer\_comes\_back},
\code{two\_hosts\_share\_a\_memory\_and\_each\_answer\_goes\_home} and
\code{four\_peripherals\_share\_one\_node}, in
\code{//lib/parts:parts\_test}.
\item \code{a\_core\_draws\_a\_solid\_and\_a\_gpu\_fills\_it}, in
\code{//soc:demo\_test}, runs a core, a GPU, a memory and a serial port
on a two by two lattice of nodes.
\item No waveform in \code{docs/BUILD.bazel} lowers the switch, so its
netlist has no co-simulation under nvc or Verilator.
\code{//docs:showcase} lists the network's netlists as not yet
simulated.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Used by}

\code{Node}, which holds two: \code{req} for requests and \code{rsp}
for responses.

\subsection*{Limits}

The switch moves one packet per cycle, not one per port. Its arbiter
is a fixed priority, and \code{//docs:noc} names a fair arbiter as the
next thing to write. \code{//docs:noc} measures the cost on the
system of \code{//soc}: the rasteriser draws its list in 36\,516 cycles
on a direct link and in 78\,391 cycles across the lattice.
