\section{An Asynchronous FIFO}
\label{sec:x-fifo}

The producer port is in one clock and the consumer port in another,
and the two stand in a stated relation: the write clock has period 4,
the read clock period 6 and phase 2, in ticks, the unit the design
shares.
The FIFO between them is \code{ChanCdc} from \code{//lib/parts}, providing
the dedicated crossing path for transactions across asynchronous clock domains
(issue 1017). A dual-clock storage array of four words is written on the
producer clock and read on the consumer clock. Write and read pointers are
Gray-coded and transferred to the destination domain through two-stage
flip-flop synchronizers; Gray-code sequencing ensures safe domain transitions.
The channel handshake enforces backpressure directly: the producer stalls
when the channel is not ready, the consumer waits for valid data words, and
the trace confirms that the producer idles whenever the FIFO fills.
