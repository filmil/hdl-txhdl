\section{An Asynchronous FIFO}
\label{sec:x-fifo}

The producer port is in one clock and the consumer port in another,
and the two stand in a stated relation: the write clock has period 4,
the read clock period 6 and phase 2, in ticks, the unit the design
shares.
The FIFO between them is \code{ChanCdc} from \code{//lib/parts}, the
one way a value crosses from one clock to another (issue 1017): a
memory of four words written on the producer's clock and read on the
consumer's, as a dual-port RAM is, and each side's pointer Gray coded
and carried to the other through two flip-flops of the other's clock,
which is what a synchroniser does; the pointer discipline is what makes
the memory's two clocks safe. The channel's own handshake is the
backpressure: the producer waits until the
channel is ready, the consumer waits for a word, and the trace shows
the producer with nothing to do at the step the FIFO is full.
