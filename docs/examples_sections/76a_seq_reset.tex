\section{A Process of Several Waits under Reset}
\label{sec:x-seq-reset}

In the netlist of Section~\ref{sec:x-seq} the reset puts
\code{at\_wait} back to its first wait, like every other register: a
reset is a restart, and a process caught half way through its
sequence begins it again. The run has to do the same, or the two part
company at the first reset that does not come at the top of the loop
and never meet again (issue 1883).

A Rust future cannot be rewound to its first wait, so the run restarts
the loop instead. \code{\#[lower]} runs each turn of a loop of several
waits through \code{comp::restart\_on\_reset}. While the reset is
asserted, the turn is dropped where it stands, and nothing it was
about to do happens. Once the reset is released, the loop begins a
fresh turn, at its first wait. A turn the reset never touches runs as
it was written.

Two kinds of loop are left as they were written. A loop of one wait is
one clocked block with no \code{at\_wait}, and its run already agreed
with its netlist under the reset. A unit that declares \code{rst}
answers the reset in its own body, and its netlist adds no clearing of
its own (Section~\ref{sec:x-reset}).

The stepper below is Section~\ref{sec:x-seq}'s, reset twice.
The first reset, of two cycles, comes while it waits for \code{go},
after it has taken 5 and added one: its third wait. The reset clears
\code{held}, empties the word channel of the 40 and the 200 it had
not taken, and sends the stepper back to wait for a word, so the
\code{go} that follows finds nothing to offer. Before this change the
run stayed at its third wait and sent the cleared \code{held}, 0,
while the netlist waited for a word.
Then 77 comes, and the stepper takes it, adds one, and sends 78 at the
next \code{go}, so the restart lost nothing but the turn it cut short.
The second reset, of one cycle, comes just after it has taken 9, at
its second wait, and sends it back to the first in the same way.
The build checks the
netlist against this run under nvc and Verilator, through the reset
and out the other side.
