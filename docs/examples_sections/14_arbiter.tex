\section{A Round-Robin Arbiter}
\label{sec:x-arbiter}

Two producers, one consumer, and a wait on either of two inputs. The
arbiter's event is \code{until} on a condition that either input
offers and the output is ready; its turn register is read after the
wait, at that edge, and says which side is served when both offer.
The side served passes the turn. The first draft read the turn before
the wait, to fix the order of a race between the two channel waits,
and that read happened in the previous step, before the step's own
drive of the turn had committed: it saw the previous cycle's turn and
granted A twice in a row. The wait comes first; state is read after
it. Producer A offers every cycle and producer B every third one,
which the trace shows: A and B alternate while B has something to
offer, and A alone is granted in between.
