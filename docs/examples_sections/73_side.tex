\section{A Side From Another Crate, Passed Whole}
\label{sec:x-side}

\code{LitePort}, the five channels a peripheral holds on an AXI-Lite
link, is a struct of ports declared in \code{txhdl\_parts}. A unit in
any crate may take it as one side of \code{run}, and its netlist has
the five ports, each named for the side and the field. A unit of
units could take it as well, and pass its fields to a child one by
one, but not the whole side: \code{\#[lower]} reads the parent's file,
and the fields of a struct declared in another cannot be seen there,
so it could not say which of the parent's ports joins which of the
child's.

Now it does not have to. When the parent is lowered, the side's ports
are listed from the \code{Ports} the struct derives, in the order the
struct declares them, and the parent's are joined to the child's in
that order. \code{self.gpio.run(bus, ..)} is one join of five
channels, and nothing in the parent names them. Whether each channel
is joined to exactly one child is still checked, when the parent is
lowered, since only then are the channels' kinds known.

The run below is \code{ex\_gpio}'s with the pins inside a unit of
units that takes the link whole and passes it on whole. The build
simulates the parent's netlist against it under nvc and Verilator.
