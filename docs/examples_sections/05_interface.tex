\section{An Interface}
\label{sec:x-interface}

An interface is declared once, with its members and its roles, and
the roles are generated: one struct each, holding the end of every
member that the role takes, and a constructor that splits each member
between them. A role that omits a member, or two roles that both drive
one, are refused by the macro with a message that names them; those
two refusals are probes in the language article, not examples here.
