{\LARGE\bfseries TxHDL cheat sheet}\hfill{\small a hardware description language that is a Rust library; every construct Rust already has was deleted, and this is what is left}}}
\vspace{4pt}

\begin{multicols}{3}

\head{The rule}
\textbf{Delete every construct Rust provides.} A transaction is a
\code{struct}; a unit is a \code{struct} that implements
\code{Unit<In, Out>}; a pipeline is an \code{async fn}; a
configuration is a trait with associated types and constants; a
clock is a type. \code{rustc} is the parser, and the type checker
finds two drivers on a wire (a move error), a clock-domain crossing
without a \code{Crossing} (a type error) and a cycle count in a
combinational body (no \code{.await} to write).

\head{Time}
A process \textbf{waits for an event, then reads state}. The waits
are \code{C::rising()}, \code{C::falling()}, \code{rx.wait()} and
\code{until(C::rising, || cond)}. Reads are plain: \code{reg.get()}
is what the last edge latched. Drives commit at the end of the tick.
\textbf{Every \code{.await} is a cycle boundary}; events that share an
edge are written \code{parallel!(a, b).await}. Time runs in ticks,
two per cycle, so a falling edge is a tick of its own.

\head{Vocabulary}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.47\linewidth}p{0.5\linewidth}@{}}
\code{Bit}, \code{Logic}, \code{U<N>}, \code{I<N>} & values; \code{Logic} is IEEE 1164's nine \\
\code{\#[derive(Transaction)]} & a struct that moves over a channel \\
\code{Reg<T, C>} & state, in clock \code{C}; \code{get}, \code{set} \\
\code{signal()} $\to$ \code{(Out, In)} & a wire's two ends; \code{Out} is not \code{Clone} \\
\code{chan()} $\to$ \code{(Tx, Rx)} & a channel, a buffer of two; \code{send}, \code{ready}, \code{wait} \\
\code{Crossing<T, A, B>} & the only way between clocks \\
\code{interface!} & members and roles, a struct per role \\
\code{when!(c => \{ r <= v \})} & a predicated drive; not a branch \\
\code{case!(v => \{ p => \{..\} \})} & many arms, first match wins \\
\code{select!(v => \{ p => e, .. \})} & a value by pattern; a mux chain \\
\code{Mem<T, N>} & words; \code{read}, \code{at(a) <= v} \\
\code{pipeline::drive(f, rx, tx)} & one invocation of \code{f} per cycle \\
\code{config! \{ Fpga: C for Top<Fpga> \{..\} \}} & a build, as one item \\
\code{\#[derive(Trace)]}, \code{Wave} & signals named from fields; VCD or FST \\
\code{\#[lower]}, \code{\#[pipeline(..)]} & a netlist from \code{run} or an \code{async fn}; a \code{fn} inlined \\
\end{tabular}
